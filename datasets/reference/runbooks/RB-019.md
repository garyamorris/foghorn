# RB-019: CPU saturation and capacity

## Triage
1. Compare rps with cpu_pct: a rise in both is load, cpu without traffic is a regression.

## Mitigation
1. `scale_out` the component (medium risk).
2. A restart helps only for runaway threads.

## Verification
1. cpu_pct under 75 percent.

## Escalation
Capacity review ticket.
