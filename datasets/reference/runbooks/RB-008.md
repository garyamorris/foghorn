# RB-008: Long-running queries and lock contention

## Triage
1. List the longest active queries and their owners; an ad hoc report or batch export started shortly before the onset is typical.
2. Confirm callers degrade after the database does.

## Mitigation
1. Terminate the offending sessions with `kill_queries`. During the peak-trading freeze this needs break-glass (POL-003): a reason of at least 20 characters and a second approver.
2. Do not restart the primary: killing the query is enough and keeps connections alive.

## Verification
1. orders-db cpu_pct and p95_ms back to baseline within 5 minutes.

## Escalation
DBA on-call; engineering manager of the query owner.
