# RB-016: Primary database failover

## Triage
1. Confirm the primary is truly unreachable (not just slow) and that a replica is in sync.

## Mitigation
1. `failover_db_primary` promotes a replica: high risk, two approvers including a DBA; break-glass during the freeze.

## Verification
1. Writes succeed on the new primary; replication re-established.

## Escalation
Incident commander and DBA lead.
