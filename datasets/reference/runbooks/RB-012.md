# RB-012: Queue consumer lag and poison messages

## Triage
1. Check consumer_lag together with the consumer's cpu_pct and rps: lag rising while throughput is near zero means a stuck consumer.
2. Find the repeated error for one offset in the consumer logs.

## Mitigation
1. Move the poison message aside with `quarantine_message`; the backlog drains without further action.
2. `pause_consumer` only stops the retry loop, it does not drain the lag.

## Verification
1. consumer_lag trending to baseline within 15 minutes.

## Escalation
Messaging on-call.
