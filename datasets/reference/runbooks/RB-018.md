# RB-018: Planned maintenance and alert suppression

## Triage
1. Check the change feed for a maintenance.window.started covering the component at the alert time.
2. Confirm the alerts only concern the component under maintenance.

## Mitigation
1. `suppress_alert` for the component for the remainder of the window. No incident is opened.
2. Anything outside the window or on another component is a real alert.

## Verification
1. Alerts resolve when the window ends; if they do not, open an incident.

## Escalation
Maintenance owner if the window overruns by more than 15 minutes.
