# RB-003: Checkout error budget burn

## Triage
1. Read the checkout error_rate and the call path: cart-service, payments-service, orders-service, inventory-service.
2. Find which callee degraded first; the failing callee, not checkout itself, is usually the cause.
3. Check recent changes on checkout and on every callee.

## Mitigation
1. Apply the runbook of the failing callee (RB-001, RB-002, RB-004, RB-007): `rollback_deploy` after a bad release, `revert_flag` after a flag ramp.
2. Publish a status update with `post_status_update` when checkout error_rate stays above 2 percent for 10 minutes; `scale_out` helps only with plain load.

## Verification
1. Checkout error_rate under 0.5 percent for 10 minutes.

## Escalation
Incident commander joins for SEV1 and SEV2 (`escalate_human`).
