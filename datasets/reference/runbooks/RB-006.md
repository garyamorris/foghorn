# RB-006: Disk full and log volume growth

## Triage
1. Confirm disk_pct is rising on one component and that the growth is log files (look for log rotation failures).

## Mitigation
1. `rotate_logs` is low risk and needs no approval: compress and drop old logs.
2. `expand_volume` is a second step when growth continues; it needs one approver.

## Verification
1. disk_pct under 80 percent and write errors gone.

## Escalation
Open a ticket for the noisy log source.
