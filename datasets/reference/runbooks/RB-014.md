# RB-014: Zone network partition

## Triage
1. Group the alerts by zone label and by minute: one network fault fans out into dozens of alerts and is ONE incident.
2. No deploy explains simultaneous failures across unrelated services.

## Mitigation
1. `drain_zone` the affected zone out of the load balancers (high risk, two approvers), or `failover_dns` to the healthy zone.
2. Do not restart or roll back individual services.

## Verification
1. Error rates in the remaining zone are at baseline; alerts resolve.

## Escalation
Network on-call.
