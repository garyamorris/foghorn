# RB-010: Cache hit ratio collapse and stampede

## Triage
1. Check hit_ratio on sessions-cache and the time of the last config change or scheduled flush.
2. Database saturation that rises together with falling hit_ratio is a symptom, not the cause.

## Mitigation
1. Revert the TTL or flush schedule with `revert_config`, and run `warm_cache` first or immediately after: reverting alone leaves a cold cache for about 6 minutes.
2. Stagger the weekly flush by shard so the stampede cannot recur.

## Verification
1. hit_ratio above 0.9 and orders-db saturation_pct back under 70.

## Escalation
Data-infra on-call; open a ticket to stagger the flush job.
