# RB-020: Latency regression triage: inherited from a dependency

## Triage
1. Walk the call graph downward from the slow component to the first callee whose own latency rose first.
2. The earliest degraded callee is the origin; callers merely inherit its latency.

## Mitigation
1. Apply the runbook of the origin component: `revert_flag` for a recent ramp, `scale_out` for pure load, `escalate_human` when the origin is unclear.

## Verification
1. Inherited latency in callers disappears after the origin recovers.

## Escalation
Origin component's on-call.
