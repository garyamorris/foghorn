# RB-002: Feature flag or configuration regression

## Triage
1. List changes in the last 4 hours and find flag.changed and config.changed entries on the failing component.
2. A flag ramp above 25 percent that coincides with the onset is the prime suspect; ask the flag owner afterwards, revert first.
3. When two changes sit close to the onset, prefer the one on the failing call path and ignore the one whose own metrics are unchanged.

## Mitigation
1. Revert the flag with `revert_flag` or the key with `revert_config`.
2. If the owner cannot be reached and customers are affected, `scale_out` is an acceptable partial mitigation.

## Verification
1. Latency and error rate of the component and its callers recover within 10 minutes of the revert.

## Escalation
Ask the flag owner whether the ramp was intended before reverting a flag that is part of a launch.
