# RB-015: Telemetry gap: metrics flatlined or missing

## Triage
1. Flat, exactly constant series are a collection fault, not a calm system.
2. Without data you cannot establish a cause; do not guess.

## Mitigation
1. `escalate_human` to the service on-call and ask them for direct evidence.
2. Do not change anything on a component you cannot observe.

## Verification
1. Telemetry resumes; resume the normal investigation.

## Escalation
Platform on-call restores the exporter.
