# RB-013: Third-party dependency outage

## Triage
1. Establish that our own components are healthy and only the external dependency fails.
2. Check for slow-but-working provider behaviour before declaring an outage.

## Mitigation
1. Fail over with `enable_fallback_provider` when a fallback exists (high risk, two approvers).
2. Keep customers informed with `post_status_update`.

## Verification
1. Success rate recovers on the fallback.

## Escalation
Vendor support ticket via `open_ticket`.
