# RB-021: Email and notification delivery failures

## Triage
1. Email provider latency alone does not stop the consumer: sends are asynchronous. Check consumer_lag before blaming the provider.

## Mitigation
1. Use the fallback provider only when the provider error_rate is high.
2. `pause_consumer` to stop a retry storm.

## Verification
1. Delivery rate back to baseline.

## Escalation
Messaging on-call.
