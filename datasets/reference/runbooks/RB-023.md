# RB-023: Alert storm handling

## Triage
1. Cluster alerts by time, zone and topology; a storm usually has one cause.
2. Do not suppress alerts before the cause is known.

## Mitigation
1. Open one incident for the cluster and follow the runbook of its cause: `escalate_human` brings in the incident commander and `open_ticket` tracks the alert-rule noise. Do not `suppress_alert` before the cause is known.

## Verification
1. Alert rate falls below 5 per minute.

## Escalation
Incident commander.
