# RB-011: Memory leak and out-of-memory restarts

## Triage
1. Plot mem_pct over several hours: a leak is a slow monotonic climb with sawtooth drops, not a step.
2. The offending release can be days old: widen the change lookback to 7 days before concluding there is no change.

## Mitigation
1. `restart_service` is a stopgap that resets memory but not the cause.
2. `rollback_deploy` of the leaking release is the real fix; open a ticket either way.

## Verification
1. mem_pct back under 70 percent; watch for relapse over the next hours.

## Escalation
Owning team opens a profiling ticket.
