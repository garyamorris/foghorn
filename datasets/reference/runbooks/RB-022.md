# RB-022: Status page and customer communications

## Triage
1. Confirm customer impact from metrics or reports before publishing.

## Mitigation
1. `post_status_update`: state what customers see (checkout, payments, search), keep it under 600 characters, never speculate about cause, never say all clear before recovery is verified.

## Verification
1. Publish the resolution update after recovery is verified.

## Escalation
Support lead.
