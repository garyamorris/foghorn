# RB-004: Payment failures and payment provider degradation

## Triage
1. Compare payment-provider error_rate and p95_ms with payments-service; if the provider is failing, nothing local needs to be rolled back.
2. Check the vendor status feed and whether a deploy or certificate change happened on payments-service.

## Mitigation
1. Switch traffic with `enable_fallback_provider` (high risk, two approvers).
2. Draft a status page update mentioning payments and checkout, never speculate about card data.

## Verification
1. Authorisation success rate above 99 percent for 10 minutes.

## Escalation
Payments lead approves the fallback; commander approves communications.
